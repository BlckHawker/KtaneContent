On the Subject of Binary Puzzle Plus

Your flavor text should go here.

If the module consists of buttons that are red and green with no black circles in between, you are looking at Binary Puzzle.

  • Clicking on a square in the 6×6 grid will toggle it between 0, 1, and unfilled.
  • Press “Reset” to revert the module to its initial state.
  • Fill all the squares with 0s and 1s in such a way that:
    1. no more than two equal numbers are next to or below each other;
    2. each row and each column contains an equal number of zeros and ones;
  • In the mod settings, the following can be changed:
    1. The puzzle dimension can be 8x8 or 10x10;
    2. each row is unique and each column is unique.;
